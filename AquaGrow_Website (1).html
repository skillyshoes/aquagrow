01 / The idea

Why AquaGrow?

Plants need the right amount of water. Too much or too little can affect their growth, while checking and watering them manually takes time. AquaGrow uses sensors and automation to make plant care smarter and more water-conscious.

💧

The challenge

Manual watering can be inconsistent, and plants may not always receive the amount of water they need.

🧠

The solution

A microcontroller checks sensor readings and controls a small pump according to soil moisture.

📱

Connected care

A mobile app displays live readings and includes a button for manual watering.

02 / The process

How does it work?

AquaGrow brings sensing, decision-making and watering together in one simple cycle.

  1. The soil moisture sensor measures the moisture level in the soil.
  2. The DHT11 sensor measures the surrounding air temperature and humidity.
  3. The ESP32 reads and processes the sensor information.
  4. When the soil is too dry, the ESP32 activates the relay to run the mini water pump for a controlled cycle.
  5. The mobile app displays the readings and lets the user request watering manually.

03 / The technology

What powers AquaGrow?

The project combines affordable electronic components with a custom mobile interface.

🔌

ESP32 DevKit

The main microcontroller. It reads the sensors, makes watering decisions and communicates over Wi-Fi.

🌱

FC-28 soil sensor

Measures soil moisture so the system can identify when the soil is getting dry.

🌡️

DHT11 sensor

Measures the air temperature and humidity around the plant.

⚡

Relay module

Acts as an electrically controlled switch for the mini water pump.

🚿

5V mini pump

Moves water to the plant during an automatic or manually requested watering cycle.

📲

MIT App Inventor

The mobile app shows temperature, humidity and soil readings, along with plant status and a Water Now button.
